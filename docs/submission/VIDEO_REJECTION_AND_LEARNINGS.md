# Rejected video and production learnings

**English** | [Deutsch](./VIDEO_REJECTION_AND_LEARNINGS.de.md)

## Decision

The earlier file named `FolderHome_Demo_Werbevideo.mp4` is rejected. It must
not be uploaded, linked from Devpost, described as the product demo, or used as
evidence that FolderHome works. Its related soundtrack, storyline and frame
review artifacts are local scratch outputs and are intentionally ignored by
Git.

## Why it was rejected

The render mixed previously known visuals with unrelated Roshambo audio and
did not establish one authentic FolderHome product journey. Parts that looked
like interfaces or terminal activity were not an adequate recording of the
verified application. A technically valid MP4 container is not a product
acceptance.

## Binding learnings for the replacement

- Use no recycled Roshambo music or audio fragments.
- Show the real FolderHome GUI, real local result files and the real agent
  confirmation flow; do not substitute rebuilt GUI or terminal mockups.
- Use the agent conversation as the narrative spine.
- Record stable product states instead of animating the application during a
  slow frame-by-frame capture.
- Keep the synthetic-data label visible and state every non-effect precisely:
  no email, call, external calendar action, cloud deployment or automatic
  archive unless it is actually verified.
- Review the rendered video itself: decode it, inspect scene middles and
  transitions, verify audio intelligibility and synchronization, and obtain
  human acceptance before any upload.

The replacement video is a new production based on the accepted local build;
it does not reuse the rejected render as source material.
