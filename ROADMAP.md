# ROADMAP — 36 Phases for FolderHome

**English** | [Deutsch](./ROADMAP.de.md)

**Status:** 2026-08-22  
**Competition name:** FolderHome  
**Total:** 36 phases

The overall roadmap is derived from the consolidated feature list, the vetted modules, and the existing implementation. Phases 1 through 36 are completed locally. A later integration into FolderHome-Sovereign is explicitly after the competition and does not count as a FolderHome competition phase.

## Completed

1. Integration foundation, contracts, manifests, audit and CLI  
2. FCSA-Dry-Run-Bridge  
3. Document ingest, search, topic dossier and folder report  
4. Document versions, comparison and archiving plan  
5. Family profiles and rule inheritance  
6. Document action plans and provider verification  
7. TXT/PDF transformation and bundling  
8. Type packages and ZIP output  
9. Folder states and correction learning  
10. Observation profiles and scan runs  
11. Single action, filing receipt and undo  
12. Folder cleanup plan and batch execution  
13. Observed cleanup routine  
14. Multiple watch queue  
15. Scheduler handoff  
16. Document contacts and local contact register  
17. Appointment candidates, local calendar and ICS handoff  
18. FindCall and call plugin probes  
19. Account statements, virtual accounts and subscription candidates  
20. Household and inventory  
21. Medication schedule and confirmed intake  
22. Health dossier and extractive medical report synthesis  
23. Insurance and contract cockpit  
24. Correspondence studio for letters, templates and custom letter designs  
25. Office and media studio for presentation, spreadsheet, Word, ODT, design set and business card  
26. Mail connector for controlled IMAP ingest and approved sending  
27. Calendar connectors and reminder handoffs for UpToday, Routinika and Google  
28. LLM-Note as a guided personal note service  
29. Encapsulated reuse of the extracted tax agent  
30. Local weather and newspaper desktop brief  
31. Understanding social law official notices, types and deadlines  
32. Objection, response and application drafts from profile and evidence  
33. Benefit and funding pre-screen with uncertainty and source model  
34. Law-checker integration and legal change monitor with impact candidates  
35. Shared local API, GUI and operating system account boundary  
36. Security/UX hardening, competition demo and submission‑ready package  

## Subsequent gates not counted toward the local phase total

- public repository creation and license visibility  
- video recording, review and public upload  
- AWS Builder ID and optional live demo decision  
- final Devpost review and explicit submit approval  
- later product integration into FolderHome-Sovereign  

## Scope limits

- “Planned” is not a claim that a connector, live service or provider already works.  
- Medical, legal, tax and financial functions remain administrative assistance with sources and uncertainties, not a binding professional decision.  
- Remote, sending, calendar live access, publication and DevPost submit each retain their own user gate.  
- After the competition this repository may remain online and later be branded as a reduced FolderHome variant. The premium integration into Sovereign is not pretended during the competition.
